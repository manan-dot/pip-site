Privacy Policy

Manan Vyas, MD videos and the Manan Vyas MD Uploader app. Last updated November 18, 2026.

Short version

The uploader is a private tool with one user, its owner. It does not collect, store or share information about viewers or about anyone else.

What the uploader does

It runs on the owner's own computer. With his sign-in and permission it uploads his finished videos to his YouTube channel as private videos and to his TikTok account as drafts. He reviews each one and makes it public himself.

What it accesses

YouTube: permission to upload videos, and to read the channel's name and id so the tool can confirm it is sending to the right channel. TikTok: the account's basic profile (display name and an account id) for the same check, and permission to send videos to the account's drafts. It does not read comments, messages, followers, viewers or analytics.

What it stores

The sign-in tokens issued by YouTube and TikTok, the channel name and id, and the TikTok display name and account id. These are kept in the password keychain of the owner's computer. They are not sent to any server other than YouTube's and TikTok's own, and they are not shared with or sold to anyone.

Viewers

This site and the uploader set no cookies and run no analytics. If you watch the videos on YouTube, TikTok or Instagram, those services handle your data under their own privacy policies. Please do not send personal health information in comments or messages.

Removing access and deleting data

The owner can remove the uploader's access at any time: in his Google account under Security, Third-party access, and in TikTok under Settings and privacy, Security and permissions, Apps and services. Removing access makes the stored tokens useless, and they can be deleted from the keychain. Because the tool holds no data about anyone else, there is nothing of yours for it to delete. If you have a question about this, message @mananvyasmd on Instagram.

Changes

This policy may be updated; the date above shows the latest version.

Pip's Science Lab

Real science for ages 4 to 6, made to watch together.

Each episode asks one question a young child really wants answered. Pip, our host, tries, fails in funny ways, changes one thing, and tries again, with a friend to talk it through. Along the way you'll see real footage from nature and the world around us.

  • Pause signs give grown-ups time to ask a question and let their child guess.
  • Every episode ends with a clear stop and a small mission to try at home.
  • Every science fact comes from a published source, listed in the video description.

Privacy Policy

Pip's Science Lab and the Pip Uploader app. Last updated September 26, 2026.

What this covers

Pip's Science Lab makes science videos for young children and publishes them on YouTube, and shares its short song videos on Instagram and Facebook for parents. The Pip Uploader is a small private tool the channel's owner uses to upload those videos to the channel's own YouTube channel and the channel's own Instagram account. It is not offered to anyone else.

What the uploader accesses

With the channel owner's permission, the uploader uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos, captions and thumbnails to the owner's own channel and to read that channel's ID so it uploads to the right place. It does not read, collect or store any viewer's data, and it does not access any other account.

Its use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Instagram and Facebook

With the owner's permission, the uploader uses Meta's Instagram Graph API, through the channel's own Facebook Page (Pip's Science Lab), to publish the channel's short videos as Reels on the channel's own Instagram account (@pipsciencelab) and to turn off comments on them. It reads only the Page's name and ID and the linked Instagram account's ID and username, so it posts to the right place. It does not read, collect or store any follower's or viewer's data, messages or comments, and it does not access any other Page or account.

What we store

The only things stored are the owner's sign-in tokens (for YouTube, and for the Facebook Page and Instagram account), kept in the owner's own computer keychain so uploads can run. They are never shared, sold or sent anywhere except to Google or Meta to upload the channel's own videos. The owner can revoke them at any time: YouTube at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and Facebook and Instagram in Facebook Settings under Business integrations.

Deleting your data

The uploader holds no data about anyone other than the channel's own accounts, so there is nothing of yours to delete. If you believe it holds something about you, email kidsscience28@gmail.com and it will be deleted within 30 days. The owner removes the uploader's own access by revoking its tokens as described above; the tokens are then deleted from the keychain.

Children and viewers

This website and the uploader collect no personal information from children or anyone else. The videos are marked "made for kids" on YouTube, and viewing them is governed by Google's privacy policy. Instagram and Facebook are for people 13 and older, so the channel's posts there are for parents and carers; viewing them is governed by Meta's privacy policy. This site uses no cookies, analytics or trackers.
